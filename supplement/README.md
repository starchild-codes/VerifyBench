# Supplementary Materials

The canonical camera-ready workbook is:

```text
verifybench_analysis_package_camera_ready.xlsx
```

It was regenerated from the repository's canonical score matrices, completed Coder 1/Coder 2 reliability files, and current derived result CSVs. It includes:

- an overview of primary, lenient, and operationalized headline results;
- all three complete score matrices;
- primary and operationalized reliability records;
- reliability and dimension-level agreement results;
- three-layer sensitivity results;
- the exact camera-ready weighting scenarios;
- the camera-ready Monte Carlo grid `{0.25, 0.43, 0.50, 0.75, 1.00}`;
- leave-one-dimension-out results;
- the primary-adjudication and operationalized-analysis distinctions.

The older `verifybench_analysis_package.xlsx` is preserved as a pre-camera-ready legacy artifact. It contains the earlier Monte Carlo summary and must not be cited as the final supplementary workbook.

## Source-of-truth rule

The canonical primary, lenient, and operationalized matrices are:

```text
../data/verifybench_primary_scores_canonical.csv
../data/lenient_scores.csv
../data/operationalized_scores.csv
```

Reliability results are regenerated from `../reliability/`. Sensitivity and robustness outputs are regenerated by the scripts in `../analysis/`. The workbook is a packaged view of those sources, not an independent scientific source.

The sample is purposive rather than statistically representative. It is predominantly language-focused; VHELM is deliberately included as a multimodal/foundation-model case. The study does not claim systematic multimodal coverage.
